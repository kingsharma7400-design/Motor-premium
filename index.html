📋 Motor — Policy Types

  • Package Policy — OD + TP (1 Year)
  • Bundle Policy — OD + TP (3 Years for Car / 5 Years for 2W)
  • Standard OD (SAOD) — Standalone Own Damage only
  • Liability Only — Third Party cover only

Private Car & Two Wheeler me chaaron available hain. Commercial vehicles (Taxi, Bus / PCCV, Goods Vehicle, Class D) me sirf Package aur Liability Only hote hain.

🚗 Vehicle Classes

  • Private Car — Petrol / Diesel / CNG & Electric
  • Two Wheeler — Petrol & Electric
  • Taxi / PCCV — upto 6 seats
  • Bus / PCCV — School Bus, Staff Bus, 6–17 / 17–36 / above 36 seats
  • Class D — Ambulance, Misc – Domestic (MISC D)
  • Goods Vehicle — GCCV (GVW-wise) & 3-Wheeler
